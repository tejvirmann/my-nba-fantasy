Install Hoops League
No App Store. It installs like an app: its own icon, full-screen, auto-updates.
1
Open in Safari
On iPhone this must be Safari, not Chrome.
2
Tap the Share button
The square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen.
3
Choose “Add to Home Screen”
Scroll the share sheet if you don't see it.
4
Tap Add
The Hoops League icon now sits on your home screen.
